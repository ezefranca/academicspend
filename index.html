Method

        

          

            
Data source

            

              All publication data comes from the
              OpenAlex
              API, an open catalog of the global research system. Works are fetched by matching the ORCID iD
              to author records, with cursor-based pagination up to 2,000 works.
              See OpenAlex documentation
              and their data policies.
            

          

          

            
Fee logic

            
    
              
  1. Known: OpenAlex provides an explicit APC paid or list price (from DOAJ) for the work.
    2. 
              
  2. Unknown: No fee data available from OpenAlex. We do not fabricate numbers.
    3. 
            

            

              We do not know if a waiver, discount, institutional membership, or prepay deal was applied. We only show what OpenAlex has.
            

          

          

            
Limitations

            
    
              
  • APCs vary by journal, year, article type, and negotiated agreements.
    • 
              
  • Fee waivers, institutional memberships, and discounts are not captured.
    • 
              
  • Only works with explicit APC data in OpenAlex are counted as "known".
    • 
              
  • Currency conversions use hardcoded approximate rates, not live data.
    • 
              
  • Some works may lack publisher or venue information in OpenAlex.
    • 
              
  • OpenAlex author disambiguation is imperfect. Authors with common names may have works incorrectly attributed. Use with caution.
    •
